Running Tatva on paid Gemini

What one scan costs, what a month costs, and the cheapest safe way to pay for it. Prices are Google's published rates as of 15 November 2026.

Recommendation

Gemini API, paid Tier 1, on Prepay. Load the minimum $5 (about ₹440) credit and leave auto-reload off.

For a 4–5 day demo that is more than enough: $5 covers about 330 scans, and even heavy testing uses 30–50 a day. Spending can never go past the $5: at zero balance the app simply stops scanning. There is no contract and no monthly fee.

Why paid instead of free

  • Patient privacy. On the free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products. On the paid tier it does not. Prescriptions carry health information, so this alone justifies paying.
  • Reliability. During testing the free tier returned "quota exceeded" and "high demand" errors, which forced the app onto a weaker backup model. Paid limits are far higher.
  • Cost is small. The whole demo costs less than ₹440.

Setting it up

  1. In Google AI Studio, open the project that owns Tatva's key and choose Set up billing.
  2. Pick Prepay and buy the minimum, $5.
  3. Leave auto-reload off, so the balance is the spending limit.
  4. Optionally set a monthly spend cap for the project on the Spend page.
  5. Keep the same API key. Nothing in the app or on Vercel needs to change.

Things to know

The $300 Google Cloud free trial does not cover Gemini. Accounts opened after 2 March 2026 cannot spend it on the Gemini API.
  • Prepaid credit is not refundable, so whatever is left after the demo stays in the account (it expires after 12 months). With $5 that is at most a few hundred rupees.
  • If more scans are needed mid-demo, topping up takes a minute and works straight away.
  • Billing updates about every 10 minutes, so the last few scans before a cap can go slightly over.
  • Limits apply per Google project, not per key. Extra keys in the same project do not add capacity.
